## Owner-supplied CRF450 stand-in

`public/models/dirtbike/bike.glb` is a modified display copy of the owner's uploaded `free-honda-crf-450.zip` (containing `2022 CRF 450.blend` and an FBX copy). The archive did not include an author, source URL or license document; none is invented here. Source hashes and the material/geometry conversion record are in `public/models/dirtbike/bike.json`. Original decals were replaced with solid finishes, while the fork finish remains gold. The asset represents the owner's dirtbike-related projects; neither construction of the motorcycle nor authorship of the downloaded model is attributed to Shreethan.

## Uploaded Wilson racket models

- `public/models/tennis/wilson-blade.glb`: **Tennis Racket Wilson Blade**, by [Vladislav3000111](https://sketchfab.com/Vladislav3000111). [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tennis-racket-wilson-blade-599589f98217465f960310104b7aa474), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- `public/models/tennis/wilson-racket.glb`: **Wilson Tennis Racket**, by [shakira.clancy](https://sketchfab.com/shakira.clancy). [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/wilson-tennis-racket-89cfc984eada49a4a926216c1457fbbf), Sketchfab Standard license as recorded in the uploaded GLB metadata.

Both models were supplied by the owner for the workshop. Embedded textures were resized to a maximum of 1024 pixels and recompressed as PNG; original geometry, UVs, embedded author credits and material maps are preserved. Scene code normalizes their orientation and scale and applies alternate paint finishes while retaining grip, string and lettering detail. The storage stand is built in scene code. The Babolat Pure Aero embed supplied as a reference is not included as a downloadable asset.
